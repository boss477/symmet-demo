About Symmet

Not more, but
rightly placed.

Symmet is a furniture studio built on a single idea — that a room feels calm when every object is in balance with the space around it. We design slowly, make honestly, and leave room to breathe.

Our philosophy

We design for the space between things — the quiet that lets a single piece hold a room.

Most furniture competes for attention. We do the opposite. Each Symmet piece is drawn to sit in balance with the light, the wall, and the objects already in your life. Nothing shouts; everything belongs.

That restraint is deliberate. It takes longer to design a shape that disappears into a room than one that dominates it — but it's the difference between something you notice and something you live with for thirty years.

01

Proportion first

We start from the room, not the object. Every dimension is chosen so a piece sits in scale with the space it's made for.

02

Honest materials

Solid oak, honed travertine, undyed wool. Materials left close to their natural state, chosen to age well rather than hide wear.

03

Made to keep

Built by hand in small runs and guaranteed for life. We'd rather you buy once, well, than replace often.
